Pocket Disc Golf

No forced ads. Ever.

Disc golf made for one hand and a few spare minutes. Pull back to throw (or swipe, your choice), watch your disc fade toward the basket, and chase your best score on three hand-built courses: Maple Meadows, Redwood Hollow and Coyote Canyon.

What you get

  • Pull back to throw: pull farther to throw farther, pull to a side to tilt. Prefer Swipe? It is in Settings. Hyzer, anhyzer, backhand and forehand.
  • 75 discs with real flight numbers. Rarity changes the art, never the flight.
  • No timers, no energy and no notifications.
  • Earn everything by playing. Mystery boxes open only with Box Tokens earned by playing, never sold (see the odds).
  • Optional rewarded ads and optional coin packs, only if you tap them. No banners, no pop-ups.
