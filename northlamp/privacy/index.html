J&H Works Northlamp privacy

Privacy Policy

Northlamp ("the game") is made by J&H Works LLC ("we", "us"). Effective date: November 8, 2026

The short version

  • The game plays fully offline and does not connect to the internet.
  • There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, email or Google account.
  • The game contains no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no crash-reporting software.
  • We do not collect, sell or share any information about you.

What stays on your phone

The game saves your progress on your phone: your Light, upgrades, Lore, coasts reached, your Keeper's Log, stats and settings. We cannot see any of it. If Google backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may also back up the game's save file to your own Google account; that is Google's backup, under Google's privacy policy, and we never see it. Otherwise, uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes your progress, and we cannot restore it.

"Export save" in Settings copies your save to your phone's clipboard and to a file on your phone. It sends nothing anywhere.

Who receives data

No one. The game has no internet permission, and we run no servers that receive data from it.

Children

The game is made for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game adds anything that collects or shares data (for example optional ads or purchases), we will update this page and the effective date before that version is released, and update the Google Play Data safety section to match.

This website

This website collects no data: it has no cookies, no analytics, no trackers, no ads, and loads no outside fonts or scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; like any web host, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver and protect the site (see GitHub's privacy statement). We do not receive or use that data.
