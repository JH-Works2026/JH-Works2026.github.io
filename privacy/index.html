Privacy Policy

Pocket Disc Golf ("the game") is made by J&H Works LLC ("we", "us"). Effective date: November 4, 2026

The short version

  • The game plays fully offline. There are no accounts: we never ask for your name, email or Google account.
  • Your progress is saved on your phone. Android may also back it up to your own Google account (we never see it).
  • If you choose to send feedback, we receive what you wrote, your scores for that round and basic phone details, not who you are.
  • If you choose to watch an optional ad for coins, Google AdMob shows the ad and collects some device data (below).
  • If you choose to buy a coin pack, Google Play handles the payment. Our small purchase-check server confirms the purchase is real, using a random Purchase ID that is not linked to you.
  • We do not sell your information.

What stays on your phone

The game saves your progress on your phone: scores, coins, Box Tokens, discs, bags, carts and other cosmetics you own, settings, any round in progress, and a small performance log. We cannot see any of it. Backup: if Google backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android also backs up the game's save file to your own Google account and puts it back when you reinstall the game or set up a new phone. This is Google's backup, under Google's privacy policy; we never see it. The performance log, unsent feedback and your ad choices are not backed up. Move my progress: Settings > Privacy > Move my progress makes a code (text or QR code) from your save that you can enter on another phone. This works offline and never goes through our server. If neither is used, uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes your progress, including coins you bought, and we cannot restore it.

Optional rewarded ads (Google AdMob)

Ads only appear if you tap a "watch an ad" button (for example "Double these coins" or "+75 coins"). Nothing in the game requires an ad, and the ad software does not start until you first tap one of these buttons. When it runs, Google AdMob, our ad partner, may collect and use:

  • your approximate location (worked out from your IP address);
  • app interactions (for example app launches, taps and ad views);
  • diagnostics (for example crash and performance data from the ad software);
  • device identifiers, such as your phone's advertising ID and app set ID.

Google uses this to show ads, measure them, and prevent fraud. Learn how Google uses this information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites . Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy We ask AdMob to show only ads rated for general audiences.

Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): before the first ad, the game shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform), where you choose whether ads may be personalised. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy > Privacy options. Your other choices, everywhere: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on the phone). Or simply never tap an ad button: then no ad data is collected at all.

Optional coin packs (Google Play Billing)

You can buy coin packs with real money. Coins only buy fixed, shown items; everything in the game can also be earned by playing. Mystery boxes are opened with Box Tokens, which are earned by playing only and are never sold. Google Play handles the payment; we never see your card number, billing address or Google account details. Google Play's own privacy policy applies to the payment.

Purchases and our purchase-check server

To confirm a purchase is real and to handle refunds, the game sends our purchase-check server (run for us by Cloudflare) the pack you bought, Google Play's order number and purchase token, the game version, and a random "Purchase ID" the game creates on this phone the first time you tap a buy button. The Purchase ID is not linked to your name, email, Google account or advertising ID; we also give it to Google Play with the purchase so the two records match. Our server checks the purchase with Google. If Google reports it was refunded, cancelled or charged back, the coins from that pack are removed from your balance (never below zero) the next time the game connects. Items you already bought with coins are kept. When this happens: right after a purchase, and, once you have tapped a buy button, a short refund check at most every 6 hours while you are online. If you never tap a buy button, the game never contacts this server for purchases. We keep purchase records for up to 400 days, then delete them. The purchase token itself is deleted as soon as the purchase is completed (normally within seconds, at most 3 days). We do not store your IP address. Our server keeps short technical logs for up to 3 days, which never contain purchase tokens or full Purchase IDs. We use this information only to deliver your coins and prevent fraud. Your Purchase ID is shown (shortened) in Settings > Privacy.

Feedback you send us

Sending feedback is optional: Settings > Privacy > Send feedback, or the card that appears after some rounds (you can turn the card off in Settings > Privacy > Ask for feedback after rounds). Nothing is sent unless you tap Send. When you tap Send, the game sends our server (the same Cloudflare server as the purchase check):

  • the face and tags you picked, and the text you typed (up to 500 characters; anything that looks like an email address or phone number is replaced with [removed], on your phone and again on our server);
  • for the card after a round: the course, your hole scores and par;
  • the game version, your phone's model, Android version and language;
  • a short feedback code for that message, and a random feedback ID made on this phone for feedback only (it is not your Purchase ID or advertising ID).

We do not store the feedback ID or your IP address. Each message keeps only a scrambled form of the ID that differs for every message, so messages cannot be linked to each other or to your phone; it lets this phone delete its own messages. To stop spam we also keep a scrambled daily count of messages per phone, deleted the next day. If you are offline, the message waits on your phone and is sent the next time you open the game (unsent messages are deleted after 30 days). You get a small coin thank-you for sending feedback (the first time, then at most once a week), whatever you write. Feedback is read only by our team and used only to improve the game. It is kept for 365 days, then deleted. Your last feedback code is shown in Settings > Privacy. Delete: Settings > Privacy > Delete my feedback deletes unsent messages on your phone at once, and asks our server to delete every message this phone sent. You can also email us a feedback code and we will delete that message. Rating the game opens Google Play's own review window; we never see what you rate or write there.

Links outside the game

Buttons for our Discord server, our support email or the game's Google Play page open another app or your browser. The game sends nothing to them; their own privacy policies apply.

Who receives data (service providers and partners)

  • Google AdMob and the User Messaging Platform: ads and consent (only if you tap an ad button).
  • Google Play: delivers the game and handles payments; Google Play Developer API: lets our server check a purchase.
  • Cloudflare: hosts our server (purchase checks and feedback) and its databases.

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for any purpose other than those above. If J&H Works LLC or the game is ever sold or merged, this information may pass to the new owner, who must follow this policy.

Security

All data the game sends (ads, purchases, purchase checks, feedback) travels over encrypted HTTPS connections. Our server keys are stored as encrypted secrets, never in the game or in our code.

How long data is kept

| Data | Where | How long | |---|---|---| | Game progress, coins, tokens, items, settings | your phone (and your own Google backup, if on) | until you uninstall or clear data (backup: under Google's rules) | | Unsent feedback | your phone | until sent, deleted, or 30 days | | Purchase records (pack, order number, Purchase ID, times, result) | our server (Cloudflare) | 400 days, then deleted | | Purchase token | our server | until the purchase is completed (at most 3 days) | | Feedback | our server (Cloudflare) | 365 days, then deleted | | Feedback spam counter (scrambled) | our server | until the next day | | Server technical logs | Cloudflare | 3 days | | Your IP address | not stored by us | - | | Ad data | Google | under Google's privacy policy |

Deleting your data

  • On your phone: uninstall the game or clear its storage in your phone's settings.
  • Feedback: Settings > Privacy > Delete my feedback, or email us a feedback code.
  • Purchase records: email jhworkssupport@gmail.com with the Purchase ID from Settings > Privacy. We delete the purchase records for that ID within 30 days and confirm by email. There is no account to close.
  • Ad data: use your phone's ad settings (above) or Google's tools at https://myaccount.google.com .

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, the UK or some US states), you may have the right to ask what data we hold about you, to correct or delete it, or to object to its use. Because we do not know who you are, we can only find data by the Purchase ID or feedback code you give us. Email us and we will answer within 30 days. In the EEA or UK you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

The game is made for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us feedback or bought a coin pack, email us and we will delete the related records.

Changes to this policy

If we change what the game collects, we update this page and the effective date before that version of the game is released, and update the Google Play Data safety section to match.
