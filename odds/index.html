Mystery box odds

Boxes open with Box Tokens only. Box Tokens are earned by playing. They are never sold, can't be bought with coins or real money, and don't come from ads or coin packs.

Pocket Disc Golf has two mystery boxes: the Fairway Box (a disc) and the Style Box (a flight trail). A box costs 12 Box Tokens. You earn Box Tokens by playing: 2 for every finished nine-hole round, +1 for finishing under par, +1 per hole-in-one (up to 2 a round), and 10 once after your first round. From December 8, 2026, the free Season 1 track also gives 3 Box Tokens at levels 5, 10, 20 and 25. You can hold up to 99 Box Tokens.

Every item in a box can also be bought in the shop with coins earned by playing. Rarity changes looks only. Discs with the same flight numbers fly the same.

Game version 1.0.0. Updated November 4, 2026. The same odds, in the same order, are shown in the game on each box's odds sheet (See odds), and before you open a box type for the first time.

  • Rare or better guaranteed within 10 boxes: if 9 Fairway Boxes in a row give no Rare or Legendary disc, the 10th is Rare (80%) or Legendary (20%). The counter is saved and starts again when you get a Rare or Legendary. The game shows how many boxes are left.
  • You always get something you don't own yet: the box picks a tier using the chances above, then one of the discs in that tier you don't own yet, each equally likely. So the chance of a specific disc = tier chance / number of discs in that tier you don't own yet.
  • If a whole tier is owned, you get coins instead (the last column).
  • Rarity changes looks only. Discs with the same flight numbers fly the same.
  • Every disc is also sold in the shop for coins.
  • Over many boxes, with the guarantee, this works out to about 56.4% Common, 28.2% Uncommon, 12.3% Rare and 3.1% Legendary (15.4% Rare or better; 10% without the guarantee).

Coming on February 16, 2027

When Season 1 ends, its two rewards join the boxes: the Hoarfrost disc joins the Rare tier (21 discs, 0.38% each) and the First Frost trail joins the Standard tier (3 trails, 23.33% each). Tier chances stay the same. This page will be updated that day.

What we never do

No box can be bought with real money or with coins. No timers or limited-time boxes. No prize for collecting a full set from boxes. Odds never change based on how much you play or spend.
